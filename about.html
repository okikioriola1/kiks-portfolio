02 / The way I see it

Good interfaces start with
understanding people.

I enjoy turning ideas into websites and applications that feel good to use. The details matter to me: how a layout adapts, how an interaction responds, and how someone finds their way through a product.

My experience spans frontend development, technical product work, and developer relations. That means I’m interested in both sides of the screen: building the interface, understanding the product behind it, and helping the developers working with it.
